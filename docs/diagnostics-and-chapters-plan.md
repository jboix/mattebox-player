# Diagnostics and chapters: the plan

Two controls join the composition. Chapters divide the seek bar and fill
a menu, inside `@mattebox/player`. Diagnostics is a panel over the picture
with the stats, the charts, the engine's state and the browser's support,
in a package of its own, `@mattebox/player-diagnostics`. Nothing in
`@mattebox/player-core` changes, and nothing in the engine.

## Decisions

| #  | Decision            | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1  | Where chapters live | The video's own text track of kind `chapters`, the browser's. The player's `chapters` attribute puts a hidden `<track>` on the video from a WebVTT URL, and a page can put one there itself, or call `addTextTrack`. The engine has no chapters and needs none; a native session has chapters the same way.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 2  | Who reads them      | The seek bar and the chapters menu, through one helper, `controls/chapters.ts`, so the two agree. Both re-read on the track list's `addtrack` and `removetrack`, the track's `cuechange`, and the `<track>` element's `load`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 3  | The seek bar        | One gap per boundary, cut through every layer of the track with a CSS mask, so the played and the buffered spans read per chapter, and the chapter's title in the preview above the time, with the thumbnail tile when there is one. `chapters="none"` on the seek bar leaves the track whole and the preview without a name.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4  | The menu            | `<mbx-chapters-menu>`, over the menu primitive, one item per chapter with its start time at its end, the one the playhead is in checked; a choice seeks to its start. Hidden without chapters. In the default bar after the speed menu, and in the panels row.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 5  | Chapter images      | None. WebVTT chapters carry a title, and the preview draws the thumbnail tile beside it when the thumbnails stage answers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 6  | The diagnostics     | A package of its own, `@mattebox/player-diagnostics`, because it is heavier than every other control together and most pages will not carry it. It is a control of the page's own kind: it implements the contract of guide chapter 03 from outside, takes the player's public types alone, and the engine through its public surface. The player does not know it.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 7  | Placement           | In the bar, `<mbx-diagnostics>` is a button that opens a panel above it, fitted under the picture's top and within the player's width, carrying `open` so the bar holds. Anywhere else inside the player, the panel itself, open, in flow: under native controls that is under the picture, the way the panels row is.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 8  | Pages               | Playback: the source, the handler, the video's own facts, the throughput and the rendition playing. Charts: the buffer per source buffer, the throughput, the stalls, the frames and the switches, drawn as the engine's playground draws them. Engine: the capabilities, the state, the buffers, the tracks, the ladder, DRM. Browser: the platform, the codecs and the key systems, probed once.                                                                                                                                   |
| 9  | Sampling            | Every half second while attached, panel open or not, so the counts cover the session: the kernel snapshot, the rendition playing, the trace ring through a cursor, and the video. The trace comes in as the engine's `trace` events, one entry at a time, kept slimmed in a history of the element's own; an engine that keeps a ring and emits nothing is read through a cursor. The engine emits no event for a quality switch, so a switch is a poll's diff, as in the playground.                                                |
| 10 | Native sessions     | Rule 5 stands: no diagnostics are invented. The playback page and the buffer, stall and frame charts read the video; the throughput and switch charts and the engine page say there is no engine.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11 | The report          | `element.report()` returns one plain object: the source, the playback, the engine's state, the counters, the last two minutes of samples and marks, the browser's support when probed, and the session's trace as the element kept it, slimmed as the playground slims it: a raw entry holds the whole presentation in every manifest, and five hundred of them run to megabytes. The Copy button puts it on the clipboard. Every fatal error dispatches it as a `report` event, bubbling through the player, so a page sends it on. |
| 12 | Words               | The button's name, the copy button's text and its text after a copy are attributes: `label`, `label-copy`, `label-copied`. The pages' keys and the tab names are technical English and have no attributes: the panel is for an integrator, and a viewer never opens it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 13 | Tokens              | The panel reads the player's `--mbx-*` custom properties, and adds `--mbx-chart-1` to `--mbx-chart-5` with defaults, for the charts' series.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 14 | Budget              | The diagnostics CDN bundle has its own budget, 20 kB min+brotli, measured at 13.1 kB. The player's holds at 30 kB: the chapters take it from 23.7 to 24.8 kB.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

## What the player gained for this

- The `chapters` attribute on `<mattebox-player>`, and `crossorigin`
  forwarded onto the video, because a chapters track from another origin
  loads only with it.
- A menu item may carry a detail at its end, `item-detail`, which the
  chapters menu uses for the start times.
- The seek bar's `preview-title` part.

## What the contract taught

The diagnostics element is the first control written outside the player
package, and the contract held: the player lookup is forty lines, the
events and the properties are enough, and nothing internal was needed.
Two things it copies instead of sharing: the button's style, and the
popup fitting under the picture's top. Both are the price of a package
boundary, and both are small.
